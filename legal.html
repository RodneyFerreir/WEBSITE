Affiliate Disclaimer

Our Promise to You

We only recommend products and services that our team has personally tested, thoroughly researched, or genuinely believes will add value to your life. Your trust is more valuable to us than any commission.

What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on Porlah are "affiliate links." This means that if you click on the link and purchase an item, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. These commissions help us maintain and improve our content curation process.

How We Choose What to Recommend

Our editorial process is completely independent of our affiliate relationships. Here's how we ensure quality:

  • Testing First: When possible, our team personally tests products before recommending them
  • Research-Based: We thoroughly research products, reading reviews, comparing alternatives, and analyzing value
  • Expert Input: We consult with domain experts and industry professionals
  • Community Feedback: We consider feedback from our community of readers
  • Value-Focused: We only recommend products that offer genuine value relative to their cost

Transparency Standards

We clearly mark affiliate content in the following ways:

  • Affiliate disclosure notices at the top of articles containing affiliate links
  • Visual indicators (🛍️ icon) next to affiliate links and product recommendations
  • "Contains affiliate links" labels on relevant content
  • Clear disclosure in our newsletter when featuring affiliate products

Editorial Independence

Our content decisions are never influenced by potential affiliate earnings. We maintain strict editorial independence by:

  • Creating content based on reader value, not commission potential
  • Recommending the best products in each category, regardless of affiliate status
  • Including non-affiliate alternatives when they're superior options
  • Regularly reviewing and updating recommendations based on new information

Your Rights

As our reader, you have the right to:

  • Know when content contains affiliate links (we'll always tell you)
  • Receive honest, unbiased recommendations
  • Contact us with questions about any product or recommendation
  • Expect that we'll update or remove recommendations if they no longer meet our standards

Questions About Our Recommendations?

If you have questions about any product we recommend or our affiliate relationships, please contact us at transparency@porlah.com.
